🍃 A little letter for you

Happy birthday Manki!! 🎂🥳❤️ I really wanted to write you a proper birthday message because you are honestly one of my favourite people in my whole life and one of the friends I really really appreciate. You have been with me through so many things. Not only when everything is good, but also when I was having hard times, stressing about things, overthinking everything and basically being a pain in the ass 😂 You still stayed with me and listened to all my stupid problems. Especially during the whole Fu situation, you were there with me every single time. You already know how much I was overthinking and how difficult that time was for me. I probably talked about the same thing 100 times 😂 but you still listened to me and stayed beside me. Honestly, not everyone does that. There are a lot of people we meet in life, but only some people actually stay when things aren't going well. And you are definitely one of those people for me. I am really lucky to have you as my friend. Thank you for always listening to me, talking with me, making me laugh and dealing with my nonsense 😂 Thank you for being there even when I wasn't in the best mood and didn't even know what I was doing with my life. I really hope you know how much I appreciate you. I might not always say it, but you are genuinely a very important person in my life. For your birthday, I just hope you get everything you want in life. I hope this year brings you lots of happiness, good health, good people, good memories and many fun moments. I hope you can achieve all the things you want to achieve and have more chances to enjoy life. You deserve good things and I really hope they come to you. And don't think I'm just saying this because it's your birthday 😂 I really mean it. Also, I WILL COME VISIT YOU 😂 So don't make other plans when I come. We are going to have a lot of fun, eat a lot, go somewhere, talk about random shit and probably make some stupid memories together 😂❤️ We still have plenty of time to make more memories, so I'm looking forward to that. I hope we stay friends for a very long time. Even if life gets busy and things change, I hope we can always talk like we do now. Anyway, enough of my emotional speech 😂 Happy birthday again, Manki!! 🎂🥳❤️ Enjoy your day properly, eat something nice, have fun and don't get too drunk 😂 Thank you for being one of the people who has always been there for me. I'm really grateful to have you as my friend. Have the best birthday, Manki ❤️🎂🥳 And yes, I'm still coming to visit you. You can't escape 😂❤️

— Your Frog 🐸
